Stress & Strain

σ = F / ANormal (axial) stressA = cross-sectional area
ε = δ / LNormal strainδ = elongation, L = original length
σ = EεHooke's Law (elastic region)valid below yield
τ = F / AShear stressA = shear area
G = E / [2(1+ν)]Shear modulus from E, νν = Poisson's ratio
FoS = σ_yield / σ_appliedFactor of safetyuse UTS instead of σ_yield for brittle materials
δ = αLΔTThermal expansionα = coefficient of thermal expansion

Torsion

τ = Tr / JShear stress in circular shaftr = radius to point of interest
θ = TL / GJAngle of twistθ in radians
J_solid = πd⁴/32Polar moment, solid shaft

Columns & Buckling

P_cr = π²EI / (KL)²Euler critical buckling loadK: 2 (fixed-free), 1 (pinned-pinned), 0.7 (fixed-pinned), 0.5 (fixed-fixed)
σ_cr = π²E / (KL/r)²Critical stressr = radius of gyration = √(I/A)
