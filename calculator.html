Does Laplace solve every ODE? No. Laplace turns d/dt into multiplication by s, which only works cleanly on linear terms — nonlinear terms (y², y·y′, sin y, …) don't reduce to simple algebra. It's a tool for linear ODEs (constant, and some variable, coefficients), not a universal solver. For nonlinear equations you're generally looking at numerical integration, phase-plane analysis, or perturbation methods. The two solvers below reflect that split.
